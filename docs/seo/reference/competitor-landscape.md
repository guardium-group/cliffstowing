## What competitors do well

- **Tuber Towing** has the strongest programmatic SEO play. Their
  `/areas-served/` directory hosts 10+ dedicated city pages each
  ranking for `tow truck [city]`. This is the pattern Cliff's
  should emulate in Phase 2/3.
- **Action Towing** has dedicated service URLs (heavy-duty,
  flatbed, etc.) — basic but functional.
- **A+ Towing** has high blog volume (30+ posts) and ranks well
  for informational queries.
- All three have established GBP listings with active review counts.

## What Cliff's has that no competitor has

- **70+ years of brand equity** — unmatched in the Edmonton market.
- **Modern Next.js stack** — faster, cleaner mobile UX than any
  competitor.
- **Most sophisticated structured data** at the code level
  (BreadcrumbList, FAQPage, BlogPosting all properly implemented).
- **Cleaner content voice** — copy reads as if written by a human,
  not a template.

---

## The opportunity

Cliff's has the strongest brand + cleanest tech, but the worst
information architecture. Closing the IA gap (Phases 1–3) puts Cliff's
ahead of every competitor in this table — because no other company has
all three advantages simultaneously.

## Link-building intelligence

Check competitor backlink profiles (Ahrefs/Semrush) to find:

- **Easy wins:** any directory or association where multiple
  competitors are listed but Cliff's isn't — that's a hole to fill
- **Local news mentions:** if a competitor was quoted in CTV
  Edmonton or Edmonton Journal, the same outlet might quote Cliff's
- **Insurance broker partnerships:** if a competitor is listed as a
  preferred provider somewhere, pitch the same broker
