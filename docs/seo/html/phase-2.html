2.3Review acquisition program

Reviews drive ~16–20% of local-pack ranking. Velocity (new reviews arriving consistently) matters more than total count.

  • TECH
    After dispatch closes a job, fire an automated SMS or email ~1 hour later. Copy: "Thanks for choosing Cliff's. If we did the job right, would you mind leaving a quick Google review? [shortlink]"
    VERIFY Test with a fake closed job — message lands in your inbox / phone within an hour.
  • MANUAL
    Get the direct review link from the GBP dashboard ("Share profile → Get more reviews"). Wrap it in a short URL.
    VERIFY Tapping the shortlink on mobile opens the GBP review form (5-star rating + text field).
  • MANUAL
    When closing the job in person: "We'll send you a quick text — a review really helps us."
    VERIFY Spot-check 5 closed jobs in a week — at least 3 customers report being asked.
  • MANUAL
    Mention the service + city in replies for additional keyword signal. "Glad we could get your truck moved safely in Sherwood Park."
    VERIFY Response rate ≥ 90% across all reviews. Businesses replying to ≥ 80% see measurable ranking lift.
  • MANUAL
    VERIFY GBP dashboard shows count ≥ 15 and average ≥ 4.0.
