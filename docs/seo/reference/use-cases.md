# SEO Use Cases — Reference

The 6 concrete ways SEO drives revenue for Cliff's Towing. Each is tied
to a specific user search behaviour, a specific page/asset on the site,
and a specific business outcome.

---

## Use Case 1 — Own the Edmonton Local Pack

**Search behaviour:** Driver stranded on the Henday types "tow truck near me" on mobile.

**What needs to be true:** Cliff's appears in the top 3 Google Maps pins
with click-to-call visible above the fold.

**Phase:** Primarily Phase 1 (GBP setup) + Phase 2 (review velocity).

**Why it's #1:** 76% of local mobile searches result in a call or visit
within 24 hours. Towing is the textbook urgent-local-intent category.
Ranking #4 is invisible.

---

## Use Case 2 — Rank Service Pages for High-Intent Commercial Queries

**Search behaviour:** Semi driver searches "heavy duty towing Edmonton."

**What needs to be true:** A dedicated, in-depth page exists at
`/services/heavy-duty-towing` — not a buried section on `/services`.

**Phase:** Phase 1 (top 4 pages) + Phase 2 (remaining 5).

**Current gap:** All 9 services collapse to `/services#anchor`.
Google does not rank URL fragments as separate pages.

---

## Use Case 3 — Location Pages for Surrounding Communities

**Search behaviour:** Driver in St. Albert searches "tow truck St. Albert."

**What needs to be true:** A page at `/service-areas/st-albert` with that
city in the URL, H1, title, and body.

**Phase:** Phase 1 (top 4 cities) + Phase 2 (remaining 8).

**Current gap:** 12 cities declared in `siteConfig.serviceArea` but zero
landing pages.

---

## Use Case 4 — Service × Location (the compounding play)

**Search behaviour:** Fleet manager in Nisku searches "heavy duty towing Nisku."

**What needs to be true:** A page titled "Heavy Duty Towing in Nisku"
exists and ranks above generic "heavy duty towing Edmonton" for that user.

**Phase:** Phase 3 — top 32 combinations (4 services × 8 cities).

**Why it works:** Low search volume per query but near-100% conversion
intent. Most competitors don't do this well = low competition.

---

## Use Case 5 — Topical Authority via Blog

**Search behaviour:** Driver searches "how much does a tow cost in Edmonton"
or "do I have to use the tow truck the police call."

**What needs to be true:** Cliff's blog has well-written, helpful answers
that surface in People Also Ask and AI Overviews.

**Phase:** All 3 phases — 4 posts in P1, 8 in P2, 8 in P3 = 20 total.

**Current gap:** 4 posts is too thin. Competitors run 20–60.

---

## Use Case 6 — Technical SEO Hardening

**Search behaviour:** Any crawler hitting the site.

**What needs to be true:** No technical issues blocking indexing,
schema validates, Core Web Vitals pass, NAP is consistent.

**Phase:** Phase 1 (most fixes) + ongoing maintenance.

**Why it matters:** Even perfect content fails without technical hygiene.
